## Two things this table will look wrong about

**The retail period ends are in January and February, and that is correct.**
A fiscal-year *label* is a company convention, and the companies disagree:
the year ending 2024-01-31 is Walmart's FY2024, while the year ending
2024-02-03 is Target's FY2023. So the corpus window filters on period end,
which is unambiguous, and the manifest's `fy` column is only the calendar
year of that period end -- a filter convenience, never a label. The
authoritative `fy`/`fp` pair arrives with the XBRL facts in M2. The four
retailers are in the universe precisely to keep this honest.

**XOM's CIK is pinned in `universe.yaml`, and every other company's is not.**
SEC's `company_tickers.json` maps a ticker to the registrant trading under
it *today*. For XOM that is `0002115436`, an entity created in a holding-
company reorganisation whose first filing postdates this entire window;
the history sits under `0000034088`, which now carries no ticker at all.
Resolution by ticker succeeds either way -- it just returns a company with
no filings, and only the per-company coverage check in `filing corpus`
notices. A corpus-wide total never would.
